   (`expected 5 to be 2`).
2. **Instrument: "mid-session model switches" finding (review item 3).**
   `pai audit tokens session` now records every model change between
   consecutive turns with the new model's `cache_read` / `cache_creation`,
   and parses `type:"system" subtype:"model_refusal_fallback"` lines
   (`originalModel`, `fallbackModel`, `apiRefusalCategory`, `scope`).
